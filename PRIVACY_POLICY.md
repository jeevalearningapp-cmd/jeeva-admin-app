## 5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

### 5.1 Who We Share Data With

#### Payment Processors
- **Stripe** (international payments): Receives transaction data, amount, and billing address
- **Razorpay** (India payments): Receives transaction data, amount, and billing address
- **We do NOT share your card details with these services**

#### Cloud & Database Services
- **Supabase** (cloud database & authentication): Stores your account data, learning progress, and usage data
- Data stored in Supabase servers with encryption

#### Push Notification Service
- **Expo**: Receives your device push token, preferences, and notification data
- Uses this ONLY to deliver notifications

#### Email Service
- **Resend**: Sends transactional and marketing emails
- Receives your email address and email content only

#### AI Service
- **Google Gemini AI**: Powers JeevaBot chatbot
- Receives your chat messages and questions
- Google processes this data according to their privacy practices

#### Analytics (if applicable)
- Aggregated usage data sent to analytics services
- Non-personally identifiable information

### 5.2 Data Sharing Restrictions
We do NOT share your personal data with:
- Third-party marketers or advertisers
- Data brokers or aggregators
- Social media platforms
- Any party without your consent (except as required by law)

### 5.3 Business Transfers
If we are acquired, merged, or assets are sold:
- Your data may be transferred as part of the transaction

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 Rights in All Jurisdictions
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Know what data we collect
- Know how we use your data
- Request corrections to inaccurate data

### 7.2 Rights Under GDPR (EU Users)
If you're in the EU, you have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Delete your data (subject to legal obligations)
- **Restrict Processing**: Limit how we use your data
- **Data Portability**: Receive your data in machine-readable format
- **Object**: Oppose certain processing (e.g., marketing)
- **Withdraw Consent**: Cancel consent to marketing emails

### 7.3 Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)
If you're in California, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether it's sold/disclosed and to whom
- Delete personal information (with exceptions)
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 7.4 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:
**Email:** privacy@jeeva.app

Please include:
- Your full name
- Email address
- Specific request
- Account ID (if applicable)

We'll respond within 30 days (or as required by law).
