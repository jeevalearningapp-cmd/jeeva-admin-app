## 4. Manual Renewal System (IMPORTANT)

### 4.1 No Automatic Renewal

**Jeeva Learning uses a MANUAL renewal system:**
- Your subscription does NOT automatically renew after expiration
- You will NOT be charged automatically
- You have COMPLETE control over renewal decisions
- You choose WHEN and IF to renew

### 4.2 Expiry Notification

**Before Your Subscription Expires:**
- Day 5 before expiry: In-app notification
- Day 1 before expiry: Email reminder
- Expiry date: Clear notice shown in app
- Notifications include: "Renew Now" option

### 4.3 What Happens After Expiry

**On Expiry Date:**
- Premium features become restricted
- You can still access previously completed content
- JeevaBot messages: Limited to 5/day
- Voice tutoring: Unavailable
- Mock exams: Limited to 1 per week
- Downloads: Cannot download new content

**After Expiry (Unlimited):**
- You can renew anytime
- All features reactivate immediately
- Your progress and history are preserved
- No penalty or extra charge for renewing late

## 5. Subscription Changes

### 5.1 Upgrade Your Plan

You can upgrade anytime:
- **Process:** Settings → My Subscription → Upgrade
- **Effective:** Immediately
- **Prorating:** You'll be charged the difference
- **Example:** Upgrade from Monthly ($9.99) to Annual ($99.99)
  - Days remaining on monthly plan are credited
  - You pay the difference for the annual plan

### 5.2 Downgrade Your Plan

You can downgrade anytime:
- **Process:** Settings → My Subscription → Downgrade
- **Effective:** At next renewal date
- **Refund:** No refund for unused days on current plan
- **Example:** Downgrade from Annual to Monthly
  - Takes effect after current annual plan expires
  - You pay the monthly rate going forward

### 5.3 Pause/Suspend

You can temporarily pause your subscription:
- **Duration:** Up to 3 months
- **Reactivation:** You choose when to resume
- **Cost:** No charges while paused
- **Access:** Premium features are suspended

## 10. Subscription Cancellation

### 10.1 How to Cancel

**Step-by-step:**
1. Open Settings → My Subscription
2. Tap "Cancel Subscription"
3. Select reason (optional feedback)
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Receive confirmation email

### 10.2 Cancellation Effective Date

- **Effective:** End of current billing period
- **Not Immediate:** You keep access until period ends
- **Example:** Annual plan purchased Jan 1
  - Cancel anytime
  - Access continues until Dec 31
  - No charges after Dec 31

### 10.3 Reactivation

After cancellation:
- You can reactivate anytime
- Your progress is preserved
- You start a new subscription at current rates
- No penalties or surcharges
