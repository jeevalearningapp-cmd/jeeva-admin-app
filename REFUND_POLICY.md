## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when Jeeva Learning will and will not provide refunds for subscription purchases. Please read this carefully before purchasing a subscription.

---

## 2. General Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligible for Refund

You may request a refund if:
- **Within 14 days** of purchase (UK/EU cooling-off period)
- You haven't downloaded or accessed substantial content
- Payment was unauthorized or fraudulent
- Technical error caused duplicate charges
- You paid for the wrong plan/duration
- Subscription was purchased by a minor without consent
- Service was not provided as advertised

### 2.2 Not Eligible for Refund

Refunds will NOT be issued for:
- Subscriptions already used for 7+ days
- Content downloaded and consumed
- Mock exams taken or access used
- Change of mind without technical issue
- Payments made more than 30 days ago (except special cases)
- Lifetime plans (non-refundable after 14 days)
- Unused portion of cancelled subscriptions

## 3. Refund Timeline by Purchase Type

### 3.1 Within 14 Days (Cooling-Off Period)

**For purchases within the past 14 days:**

#### Unused Subscriptions
- **Status:** Full refund eligible
- **Condition:** No significant content accessed
- **Process:** 5-7 business days to refund
- **Who qualifies:** UK/EU customers (GDPR/consumer law)

#### Partially Used Subscriptions
- **Status:** Case-by-case review
- **Condition:** Minimal access/usage
- **Determining factors:** Login activity, quizzes attempted, content downloaded
- **Example:** Logged in once, didn't use any features → Full refund

#### Fully Used Subscriptions
- **Status:** NO refund
- **Condition:** Accessed multiple features
- **Example:** Took mock exams or completed learning content → No refund

### 3.2 Between 15-30 Days

**For purchases 15-30 days ago:**

#### Non-refundable
- Most subscriptions are non-refundable after 14 days
- Partial refunds not issued

#### Exception Cases
- Duplicate charges (charged twice)
- Technical errors
- Unauthorized purchases
- Service failure

### 3.3 After 30 Days

**For purchases more than 30 days ago:**

#### No Refund
- Outside refund window
- Standard policy: subscriptions are non-refundable
- No refunds for partial usage

#### Exception Cases (Contact Support)
- Billing disputes
- Fraud investigation
- Special circumstances
- Discretionary review

## 4. How to Request a Refund

### 4.1 Step-by-Step Process

**1. Submit Request**
- Go to Settings → Support → Request Refund
- OR email: refunds@jeeva.app
- Include: Name, email, subscription date, reason

**2. Provide Information**
Required details:
- Account email address
- Purchase date
- Transaction ID (if available)
- Reason for refund request
- Description of issue (if applicable)

**3. Our Review**
- We review your request within 24-48 hours
- May contact you for additional information
- Decision sent via email

**4. Refund Processing**
- If approved, refund initiated within 5-7 business days
- Refund appears in your original payment method

**If service was not provided as promised:**
- **Eligibility:** Full or partial refund based on impact

### 6.4 Minor's Purchase

**If subscription purchased by someone under 18:**
- **Parent/Guardian:** Can request full refund
- **Verification:** Age confirmation required

- **Timeline:** Within 14 days of notification

**If you didn't authorize the charge:**
- **Eligibility:** Full refund
- **Verification:** Investigation into account access
- **Timeline:** Within 5-10 business days
- **Follow-up:** Account security review and password reset

## 9. Partial Refunds

### 9.1 When Partial Refunds Are Issued

Partial refunds may be given for:
- Refund requests within 7 days with minimal usage
- Technical issues preventing access
- Service not provided as advertised

### 9.2 Partial Refund Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Partial Refund = (Days Unused / Total Days) × Total Paid

Example:
- Plan: Annual ($99.99)
- Used: 10 days
- Remaining: 355 days
- Refund: (355/365) × $99.99 = $96.84
```

## 10. UK/EU Consumer Rights

### 10.1 14-Day Cooling-Off Period (GDPR)

**If you're in the UK or EU:**
- You have 14 days from purchase to cancel
- No questions asked
- Automatic full refund for unused plans
- Refund within 14 days of cancellation

### 10.2 Requesting Refund Under EU Law

**Steps:**
1. Contact support within 14 days
2. State: "I wish to exercise my consumer cancellation rights"
3. Provide: Account email and purchase date
4. Full refund issued

### 10.3 Consumer Protection Act 2015 (UK)

You have rights regarding:
- Services provided as described
- Within a reasonable timeframe
- Of satisfactory quality
- Fit for purpose

If service fails to meet standards:
- Contact support with details
- We may fix issue or offer refund
- Your choice of remedy (repair or refund)

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Will I get a refund for my unused days?
**A:** No, subscriptions are non-refundable after 14 days. You keep access until your period ends.

### Q2: What if I accidentally purchased twice?
**A:** Contact support immediately. We'll refund the duplicate charge.

### Q3: Can I get a refund 3 months after purchase?
**A:** Generally no, unless there's a technical issue or fraud. Standard window is 30 days.

### Q4: Will I lose my learning progress if I get a refund?
**A:** If refund is approved, subscription features are disabled, but your data is preserved if you resubscribe.

### Q5: How long does a refund take?
**A:** 5-10 business days depending on payment provider.

### Q6: What if my refund doesn't appear?
**A:** Check with your bank (may take longer). Contact support if it's been 14+ days.

### Q7: Are lifetime plans refundable?
**A:** Yes, within 14 days if unused. After 14 days, no refund.
