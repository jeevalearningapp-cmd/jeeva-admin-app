## PART A: NUMERACY (15 Questions)

### Question 1

A patient is prescribed 1 g of paracetamol. Tablets available are 500 mg.  
How many tablets should be given?  
a. 1  
b. 2  
c. 3  
d. 4

**Correct answer:** b  
**Explanation:** 1 g = 1000 mg. 1000 ÷ 500 = 2 tablets.
